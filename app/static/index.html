Pipeline & Model Architecture

1. URL Feature Engineering & Random Forest

The system extracts 23 specific structural and lexical dimensions per URL, including domain Shannon entropy, digit ratios, keyword frequencies, and TLD abuse metrics. A Random Forest Classifier with 100 decision trees then aggregates sub-decisions to resist individual feature obfuscation.

2. NLP Message Preprocessing & Naive Bayes

Email and SMS text messages are cleaned with regex token normalization (replacing URLs, phone numbers, and emails with structural tokens). A TF-IDF N-Gram Vectorizer (unigrams and bigrams) paired with a Multinomial Naive Bayes classifier quantifies urgency and credential-harvesting intent.

The Anatomy of Modern Phishing Attacks

How attackers exploit subtle structural tricks and human psychology.

1. Typosquatting & Homoglyphs

Attackers register domains that look nearly identical to popular brands, replacing letters with numbers (e.g., paypa1.com) or using Punycode internationalized characters (xn--googl-rsa.com) to mimic legitimate symbols.

2. Subdomain Masquerading

In a URL like chase.com.login-verify.xyz, the actual registered domain is login-verify.xyz, not Chase! Attackers prepend brand names as subdomains to fool users glancing quickly.

3. Artificial Urgency & Coercion

Phishing messages weaponize deadlines ("Suspended in 24 hours", "Immediate wire verification required") to bypass critical thinking and trigger panic reactions.

4. Proactive Defense Strategies

Always inspect the root domain before entering credentials. Enable hardware Multi-Factor Authentication (FIDO2/WebAuthn), which cryptographically prevents phishing proxies from capturing your login session.
